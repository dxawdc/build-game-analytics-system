# Product archetypes

Choose a primary archetype, then add relevant secondary modules.

| Archetype | Player value focus | Core outcome candidates | Essential modules | Frequent failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile F2P RPG/card/gacha | Progression, collection, content mastery | Weekly meaningful progressors | onboarding, retention, monetization, economy, content, live ops | optimizing payer averages while retention/economy deteriorate |
| SLG/MMO | Long-term progression, social coordination, persistent world | Weekly socially embedded progressors | server, guild, economy, PvP/PvE, churn, live ops | ignoring server age, power bands, alliances, or whale concentration |
| Competitive PvP/esports | Fair, satisfying competition and mastery | Weekly quality-adjusted match players | matchmaking, balance, latency, integrity, rank, social | treating match count as value while stomps, queues, or toxicity rise |
| Casual/hybrid-casual | Fast repeatable fun | Retained quality sessions or completed core loops | creative funnel, first-session, retention, ads, difficulty | maximizing ad impressions at the cost of session quality |
| Ad-supported | Enjoyment sustained by acceptable ad load | Quality play time with ad guardrails | ad funnel, fill, eCPM, ad load, churn, performance | revenue lift caused by intrusive frequency |
| Premium PC/console | Purchase satisfaction and meaningful play | Purchasers reaching meaningful play milestones | purchase/refund, hardware quality, progression, completion, DLC | using DAU as the sole health measure |
| Subscription | Recurring value and renewal | Subscribers realizing value per billing cycle | trial, activation, renewal, cancel, content breadth | measuring logins without renewal intent or content satisfaction |
| Live-service seasonal | Durable re-engagement through cadence | Retained seasonal value participants | season cohorts, pass, content, events, reactivation | pre/post event attribution and content cannibalization |
| UGC/platform | Healthy creator-consumer marketplace | Satisfied consumption plus retained creation | supply, discovery, creator funnel, marketplace, safety, network | optimizing consumption while creator supply or discovery collapses |
| Sandbox/co-op | Emergent creation and social play | Weekly successful shared experiences | sessions, creation, co-op groups, social graph, reliability | counting time without distinguishing idle or failed sessions |

## Secondary trait modules

- **Server/world based**: server age, population balance, concurrency, queue, economy, migration, merge, faction balance.
- **Match based**: queue, match quality, win probability spread, abandonment, rematch, rank progression, latency.
- **Character based**: distinguish account, roster, and character metrics.
- **Content heavy**: reach, unlock, attempt, completion, stuck point, time-to-complete, replay, freshness.
- **Economy heavy**: sources/sinks, holdings, velocity, price levels, concentration, conversion paths, exploit detection.
- **Social heavy**: invite, accepted relationship, party/guild participation, group survival, network position, moderation.
- **Creator marketplace**: creator activation, publish success, discovery coverage, consumer satisfaction, creator earnings, take rate.
- **Offline capable**: sync success, clock integrity, offline/online attribution, duplicate reward protection.
- **Cross-platform**: account linking, platform mix, entitlement parity, cross-play, device migration.

## Lifecycle adaptation

### Concept or pre-production

Focus on research questions, prototype telemetry, playtest task success, comprehension, fun/satisfaction, and content hypotheses. Avoid fake precision on LTV.

### Closed test

Focus on stability, first value, task/funnel failure, qualitative feedback linked to behavior, progression pacing, balance, and small-cohort retention. Treat thresholds as directional because samples and acquisition are biased.

### Soft launch

Focus on data-contract quality, acquisition cohorts, exact retention, unit economics, monetization, crash/performance, content capacity, and experiment readiness.

### Global launch

Focus on scale, attribution, channel quality, server capacity, incident monitoring, conversion, cohort mix, and regional/platform differences.

### Growth

Focus on driver decomposition, experiments, segmentation, creative/channel quality, live ops, economy, personalization, and forecast accuracy.

### Mature or declining

Focus on core-user health, reactivation, content ROI, server/world health, concentration risk, cost-to-serve, and sustainable cadence. Do not mask cohort decay with acquisition.

## Recommended metric packs

Start with:

- one product-value outcome;
- acquisition and activation funnel;
- active population composition;
- retention cohorts;
- business-model-specific monetization;
- core-loop depth and breadth;
- quality and fairness guardrails;
- one product-specific module;
- event and data-quality coverage.

Add advanced prediction, personalization, graph, or causal models only after definitions and instrumentation are stable.
